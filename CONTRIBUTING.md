# Inspect, test, modify and report findings

Please inspect the specifications, reproduce local checks, modify separately versioned copies, and report concrete findings. Supply the component version, clause/file path, hashes, minimal synthetic reproduction, expected and observed behavior, and dependency versions. Distinguish implementation defects, specification contradictions, documentation gaps and accepted limitations.

Keep canonical snapshots immutable. Behavioral successors should include targeted regression evidence and predecessor identities. Do not convert a passing fixture into a semantic accuracy claim, rewrite raw output into compliance, or reconstruct missing historical provenance. No assessment or paid service is needed to contribute.

Use the repository's issue and pull-request facilities after publication. Do not submit private documents, credentials or identifying conversations. No private reporting address is advertised until the publisher establishes one. Contributions should identify applicable licenses and third-party origins and be offered only by people authorized to do so.
